Common questions

Pricing, billing, and the bits people ask before booking a demo.

Which plan should we start on?

Most clinics start on Growth — the AI SMS handler is where the ROI lives. Starter is a soft landing if you want to validate the alerts and routing layer first, then upgrade in-place when you're ready.

Scale is for multi-site or 8+ practitioner clinics. If you're not sure where you sit, book a demo and we'll be honest with you.

Are there per-message or per-user fees?

No. The monthly subscription includes SMS sending costs at standard Australian carrier rates. No seat limits. No surprise overages.

What happens if we outgrow our plan?

Move up at any time — pro-rated for the rest of the billing period. No call required. We'll also reach out proactively if your usage suggests a better fit.

How does the 14-day trial work?

We connect Avilo to your Cliniko, configure your routing, and run it live for 14 days. No card required. If it isn't earning its keep by day 14, you walk away — we keep nothing.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in one click from your dashboard. No notice period, no claw-back, no hassle. Subscriptions are month-to-month.

What about the features marked "Coming soon"?

Reactivation campaigns, cancellation waitlist auto-fill, and day-of patient updates are on the near-term roadmap. They ship to your plan at no extra cost as they go live — early customers help shape what gets built next.

Is Avilo a Cliniko-approved integration?

Yes — Avilo is officially integrated with Cliniko via their API. Reads availability, writes appointments, syncs notes back. No data leaves Australian-hosted infrastructure.

What about clinical safety?

Avilo doesn't answer clinical questions. The moment a patient mentions pain levels, symptoms, treatment specifics, or anything emotional, Avilo stops and escalates to the clinician. You set the confidence threshold per practice. Conservative by default.
