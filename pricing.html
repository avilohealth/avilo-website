Pricing questions

The usual ones.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Confirmations is set up by you with an API key. Smart Reminders and the Assistant are set up with us, and that's included.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Everything is month to month. Cancel in the app or by email and it ends at the close of the current billing cycle.

Do you offer refunds?

Fees are not refunded for partial months, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it. Start with the free trial so you're sure before you pay.

What if I have multiple clinics?

One subscription covers up to three locations under one Cliniko account. Beyond that, each extra location is {{PRICE_LOCATION}} a month. Separate Cliniko accounts are separate subscriptions.

Why is the Assistant priced "from"?

Because it does more for a clinic sending 2,000 messages a month than one sending 200. We size it on the demo from your real volume, and you'll have a number before you commit.
