How a chiro front desk runs

Short adjustments, a packed column, and a diary that never sits still.

A chiropractic clinic sees a lot of people in a day. Adjustments are brief, patients on a care plan come in two or three times a week in the early phase, and a single practitioner's column can hold more appointments before lunch than some clinics see all day. Every one of those appointments sends a reminder and most get a reply. The desk reads them between greeting patients and taking payments, usually with someone waiting at the counter.

The other thing about chiro is movement. Patients on a care plan treat the visit as a standing commitment and would rather move it than miss it, so same-day reschedules are constant: running late, can I come at four, can I swap to tomorrow. A reschedule buried in a list of yeses is a slot that stays blocked until someone finds it. In a day built from short slots, one unnoticed message can leave two gaps.

What matters most in chiro

Three features that pay off in a high-volume diary.

  • Colour-coded diaryA chiro column with dozens of short slots cannot be checked against a message list. Coloured by reply, the practitioner and the desk see which slots are solid and which are shaky before the day starts.
  • Patient inboxReschedule requests are the everyday reply in chiro and the easiest to miss in a flat list. The inbox puts them at the top, one thread per patient, with the Cliniko profile one click away so the move takes seconds.
  • Morning call listWith this many appointments, even a small share of non-responders is a long list. Avilo sends the desk one list at 8am, sorted by appointment time, so the first calls go to the first slots.

Questions

What chiro clinics ask.

Our patients come in two or three times a week. Will they get extra messages?

No. Confirmations sends nothing. Cliniko's reminders go out exactly as they do now and Avilo only reads the replies. If you move to Smart Reminders later, you choose which appointment types get a follow-up nudge, so a patient on a frequent care plan is not nudged before every visit.

A patient replies asking to come at a different time today. Does Avilo move the appointment?

Not on Confirmations. Avilo recognises the reply as a reschedule, flags the appointment as needing a person and puts it at the top of the inbox. Your team moves it in Cliniko. Moving appointments automatically against the live diary is what the Avilo Assistant does.

Can Avilo cancel the slot the moment someone says no, so we can fill it the same day?

Yes, if you choose that rule. A plain no cancels the appointment in Cliniko within minutes, the status is written to the appointment note, and if anyone on your Avilo waitlist matches the slot the team is alerted. A no with a question attached is always flagged rather than cancelled.

We have several chiropractors sharing one front desk. Can the lists be split?

Yes. The diary, the inbox and the morning call list all filter by practitioner, and the Monday cancelled-without-rebook list can be sorted by practitioner too. The desk can work from one combined list, or each chiropractor can check their own.
