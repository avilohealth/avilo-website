How an osteo clinic runs

Longer appointments, a smaller team, and nobody at the desk.

Osteopathy appointments are long compared with most of allied health. An initial consult can take the better part of an hour and follow-ups are not far behind, so a full day holds far fewer patients than a physio or chiro diary. Each appointment carries a larger share of the day's income, and a gap at ten in the morning cannot be closed by nudging two short slots together.

Many osteopathy clinics are one or two practitioners with no reception. The osteopath is treating when the reminder replies come in, reads them on a phone between patients, and tries to remember which ones need a call back. A no that arrives at seven in the evening is often not seen until the next morning, by which time the slot is hours away and anyone who wanted it has made other plans.

What matters most in osteo

Three features that matter when the appointments are long.

Instant cancel on a no

With long appointments, one empty hour is the largest avoidable loss in the day. Avilo can free the slot the moment the no arrives, at night or over a weekend, not when you next pick up your phone.

Waitlist offers

Osteo patients in pain want to be seen soon and will take a cancellation. Keep them on the Avilo waitlist with the days that suit them, and on Automate, Avilo AI offers them a freed slot straight away.

Monday cancelled-without-rebook list

Without a receptionist, the patient who says they will call to rebook is the one who slips. Every Monday Avilo lists last week's cancellations with nothing booked after them, so the calls get made.

I work alone. Do I have to check Avilo during the day?

No. Avilo works in the background and reaches you when it matters. The morning call list arrives by email before you start, and the status of every reply is written to the Cliniko appointment note you already look at.

A patient asks whether they should still come in with a cold or a flare-up. Who answers?

You do. Avilo recognises that the reply is a question rather than a yes or a no and marks the appointment as needing a person. Nothing about symptoms, or whether treatment should go ahead, is ever answered by Avilo AI.

Can Avilo cancel the appointment automatically if a patient says no at night?

Yes, if you turn that rule on. A plain no is cancelled in Cliniko within minutes, whatever the hour. If you would rather see every cancellation before it happens, choose to flag instead and Avilo leaves the appointment in place with a red mark.

On Automate, what if I want to answer a patient myself?

Reply in the thread and Avilo AI pauses on that conversation. It checks with you before it picks the thread back up, so you never end up talking over each other.
