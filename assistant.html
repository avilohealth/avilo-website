Knows what isn't its job

Clinical, emotional or unsure: straight to a person.

The Assistant never answers a clinical question and never guesses. Anything that looks clinical, emotional, urgent or ambiguous stops the automation and goes to the right person within a minute, full thread attached. A staff reply pauses the Assistant on that conversation.

  • Alerts on Slack, Teams or SMS, to whoever you nominate, including after hours
  • Worsening pain gets an earlier appointment offered and the practitioner briefed, not advice
  • Thresholds for clinical and urgent routing cannot be lowered

Waitlist and recalls

Empty slots filled. Lapsed patients invited back.

When a slot opens, the Assistant offers it to the matching waitlisted patients straight away; first yes takes it, everyone else is told politely. And on the schedule you set, it checks in with patients who haven't been seen in a while and books them back in.

  • Waitlist offers go out the moment a slot opens
  • Recall messages use name, last visit and practitioner, never clinical detail
  • You approve every recall campaign before it sends

Questions

What clinics ask about the Assistant.

What will the Assistant never do?

It never answers a clinical question, never gives advice, and never guesses. Anything clinical, emotional, urgent or unclear is handed to a person within a minute, with the full thread attached. If someone asks for a human, they get one.

Can my team take over a conversation?

Yes, at any time. The moment a staff member replies in a thread, the Assistant pauses on that conversation. When it resumes, it checks before sending anything.

Does it answer the phone?

After-hours and overflow voice answering is an add-on that's rolling out to Assistant clinics now. Ask on the demo and we'll tell you exactly where it's up to.

How is it priced?

Per clinic, sized to your message and call volume on the demo. Ask us and you'll have a number before you commit to anything.
