Overview

Avilo Health Pty Ltd (ACN 698 695 019) ("Avilo", "we", "us") provides a patient-communications service for allied health clinics. Depending on the plan a clinic chooses, Avilo tracks appointment confirmations, sends reminders, replies to patient text messages, answers calls with an AI assistant, fills cancelled appointments from a waitlist and checks in with patients a clinic has not seen for a while. We do this on behalf of the clinics that subscribe to us (each a "Clinic").

We handle health information, which is sensitive information under Australian privacy law, and we treat it accordingly. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles ("APPs"), and with state and territory health records laws including the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW) and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic). We commit to this in our contract with every Clinic.

This policy has three parts:

  • Part A: information we collect for ourselves (website visitors, customers, Clinic staff).
  • Part B: patient information we handle on behalf of Clinics.
  • Part C: what our software decides automatically, and who reviews it.

If you are a patient of a clinic that uses Avilo: your relationship is with your clinic, and your clinic's privacy policy applies alongside this one. Requests about your information should normally go to your clinic first, and we will help them respond. Parts B and C are written for you.

Part A: Information we collect for ourselves

A1. What we collect

  • Website and enquiries: name, email, phone, clinic name and role when you send an enquiry, join our newsletter or book a demo; technical data such as IP address, browser and pages visited, recorded in standard web server logs.
  • Customer accounts: names, work email addresses, roles and sign-in credentials of Clinic staff who use Avilo; account settings; a record of changes made to a Clinic's settings and who made them.
  • Billing: billing contacts and transaction records. Card details are held by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Support: the content of your emails, calls and support requests.

Retention. Enquiry and newsletter details are kept for 24 months after our last contact, or while you remain a customer. Web server logs are kept for 90 days. Billing records are kept for 7 years as tax law requires.

A2. Why we collect it (APP 3, APP 6)

To provide and administer the service; to support accounts; to bill; to answer enquiries; to improve the product; to send service notices; and, with consent or as the law permits, marketing you can opt out of at any time. We do not sell personal information.

Some of the providers listed in A4 and B5 handle this information overseas.

Our website sets essential cookies only, unless you agree to more in the cookie banner. With your consent we load the Calendly booking calendar, the Meta Pixel for ad measurement, and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without using cookies. Part B: Patient information we handle for Clinics

B1. What flows through Avilo

  • Identity and contact details: name, mobile number, email, date of birth and, where a patient gives it, address. These come from the Clinic's practice management system ("PMS") or from the patient directly.
  • Appointment information: times, appointment types, practitioners, locations, attendance and cancellation history, confirmation status, waitlist requests, class bookings, and the appointment note field.
  • Communication content: text messages sent and received on the Clinic's Avilo number; on the Confirmations features, patients' replies to the reminders the Clinic's own PMS sends; written transcripts and summaries of calls answered by the AI assistant; the messages Avilo sends for the Clinic.
  • Health information a patient volunteers: what a patient chooses to say when booking or messaging, for example describing an injury, or a short reason for a visit when a new patient books.

What we deliberately do not collect or hold:

  • No call audio. Calls answered by the AI assistant are transcribed as they happen. Audio recording is switched off; only the written transcript and a summary are kept.
  • No treatment notes. We do not read practitioners' clinical or treatment notes.
  • No patient payment card details.

B2. How we collect it (APP 3, APP 5)

Most patient information reaches us from the Clinic's PMS, through a connection the Clinic authorises and can revoke, or from patients messaging or calling the Clinic.

New patients. Where someone who is not yet a patient of the Clinic calls or messages, Avilo may create a patient record and book an appointment for the Clinic. In that case we collect your details, including a brief reason for your visit, directly from you, for the Clinic. Callers are told at the start of every call that they are speaking with an AI assistant. You can ask for a person at any point.

Children. Where a patient is a child, we deal with the parent or guardian whose contact details the Clinic holds.

We collect health information only where it is reasonably needed to book or manage an appointment. Please do not send clinical detail by text; anything clinical is passed to the Clinic's team and is not answered by the AI.

B3. What we do with it (APP 6)

Only what is needed to run the service for the Clinic:

  • read patient messages and replies, and work out what the patient is asking for;
  • where the Clinic has switched it on, reply to patients by text or answer calls to book, move or cancel appointments and answer the Clinic's approved practice questions;
  • write changes back to the Clinic's PMS: appointments booked, moved or cancelled, class bookings, confirmation notes on an appointment, new patient records, updated contact details, and a copy of each message in the patient's file;
  • send reminders, confirmation requests, waitlist offers and check-in messages the Clinic has set up;
  • pass to Clinic staff anything clinical, urgent or unclear, and anything where a patient asks for a person;
  • show conversations, flags and reports to the Clinic's authorised staff, in the Avilo app and through the notification channels the Clinic chooses (see B5).

We never contact a Clinic's patients for any purpose of our own, and we never use patient information for our own marketing.

B4. Reminders, check-ins and opting out (APP 7, Spam Act 2003)

  • Opting out of check-in and recall messages. Reply OPT-OUT or STOP to any check-in, recall or promotional message and you will receive no more of them from that Clinic. Opt-outs are handled by fixed rules before any AI sees the message, and take effect straight away.
  • Appointment reminders are service messages about a booking you have made, not marketing. If you would prefer not to receive them by text, tell your clinic and they will arrange another way to remind you.
  • Check-in and recall messages are sent only to a Clinic's own patients and name the Clinic. They use your name, when you were last in and who you saw. They are designed not to mention your condition, symptoms or treatment, because a text can appear on a screen someone else can see.
  • On the Confirmations features, reminders are sent by the Clinic's own PMS, not by Avilo. Avilo reads the replies.

B5. Who we disclose it to, including overseas (APP 8)

The Clinic and its chosen channels. Patient information is shown to the Clinic's authorised staff. If a Clinic chooses to receive alerts and summaries by email or in a team-messaging workspace it nominates, those alerts can include a patient's name, mobile number and the words of their message. The Clinic controls who is in those channels.

B6. Security (APP 11)

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest in our database.
  • The Clinic's PMS key is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and never shown again after it is entered.
  • Every request is tied to one Clinic on our servers, so one Clinic's staff cannot reach another Clinic's data.
  • Clinic staff sign in individually, with access set by role.
  • Changes to a Clinic's safety settings are confirmed, given a reason and logged.

Access by Avilo staff. Avilo is a small team. Our staff open a Clinic's account only to set it up, support it or fix a fault. Each time a staff member opens a Clinic's account, the session is logged with who, when and whether they could make changes, and every change is recorded.

No system is perfectly secure. Our Security page describes our controls in plain language.

B7. Data breaches

If a breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we begin assessing it immediately, notify affected Clinics without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware, and work with each Clinic to meet the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, including notifying the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals where required.

B8. Access, correction, retention (APP 11.2, APP 12, APP 13)

  • Patients: contact your clinic first. If you contact us at privacy@avilo.com.au we will verify who you are, coordinate with your clinic and respond within 30 days.
  • While a Clinic is a customer: conversation records, call transcripts and message history are kept so the Clinic's team can see what was said. A copy of each message is also written to the patient's file in the Clinic's own PMS, which the Clinic keeps under its own record-keeping obligations. On the Confirmations features, Avilo's working copy of the appointment diary is cleared each night once the appointment day has passed.
  • On request: we delete or de-identify an individual patient's information held by Avilo within 20 business days of the Clinic's written request, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • When a Clinic leaves: the Clinic may ask for an export within 30 days. We delete the Clinic's patient information from our systems within 90 days of the subscription ending. Backups expire on our database provider's normal rotation.
  • No call audio is kept at any time.

Part C: What our software decides, and who reviews it

This part describes how computer programs use personal information to make, or help make, decisions that could affect a patient (APP 1.7 to 1.9).

C1. What information the software uses

The content of your message or call, your contact details, and your appointment details at the Clinic: the categories in B1.

C2. Decisions made by software without a person

  • Whether Avilo answers or a person does. When a message or call arrives, software works out what you appear to want. Routine requests (booking, moving, cancelling, a practical question) can be handled by Avilo where the Clinic has switched that on. Anything clinical, urgent or unclear, and any request for a person, is passed to the Clinic's team. Fixed rules, not AI judgement, catch opt-outs and clinical content first.
  • Booking, moving and cancelling at your request. Where you ask, Avilo can book, move or cancel your appointment. Which times exist is read from the Clinic's diary by ordinary programmed rules. A cancellation or move is confirmed back to you.
  • Cancelling after a "no" to a reminder. Where a Clinic has switched this on, software cancels an appointment in the Clinic's PMS when a patient clearly replies "no" to a reminder more than 24 hours before the appointment. A late "no" is never cancelled automatically; it is flagged to staff instead.
  • Offering freed appointments. When an appointment is cancelled, software can offer the time to patients on the Clinic's waitlist whose request matches.
  • Second reminders. Software decides which patients have not confirmed and can send, or cause the Clinic's PMS to send, a second reminder.

C3. Decisions where software helps a person

  • Check-in and recall messages. Software lists patients a Clinic has not seen for a period the Clinic sets. A person at the Clinic approves these messages before they send. A Clinic can also switch on a short follow-up text that is sent automatically a few days after an appointment. It asks how you are going and does not offer appointments.
  • Flags and lists. Software sorts replies into lists for staff (confirmed, not coming, needs a reply). Staff decide what to do.

C4. Limits we hold to

  • Avilo does not give clinical advice, and anything that looks clinical or urgent is passed to the Clinic's team. Avilo is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 000.
  • Clinic staff can take over any conversation at any time. A staff reply pauses Avilo on that conversation.
  • Patient content is never used to train AI models. We use our AI providers under business terms that do not permit them to train on it.
  • Patient content is never shared between Clinics. General improvements to Avilo use de-identified, aggregated information only.

C5. If you disagree with how something was handled

Tell your clinic, or contact us at privacy@avilo.com.au. A person will review what happened. The full conversation is available to your clinic.

Complaints and contact

Privacy questions or complaints: privacy@avilo.com.au. We acknowledge within 7 days and respond within 30 days.

Not satisfied? You can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992.

Changes

Material changes are notified to Clinics by email at least 30 days before they take effect. The current version is always at avilo.com.au/privacy.
