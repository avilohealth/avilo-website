03

The problem

A cancellation leaves a gap nobody fills.

Someone is meant to offer the time to another patient or ring around the waitlist. A busy front-desk often struggles to ring in time because patients in the clinic come first.

The fix · Avilo AI

From $139/month →

Rebooked by text, then offered to the waitlist.

Avilo offers the patient a new time and moves the booking. If the slot is still free, it texts matching patients on your waitlist and books whoever says yes first.

Hi, can't make Thursday 10am sorry, something's come up
No problem. Sam has Thursday 4:30pm or Friday 8:15am. Would either work?
Friday works
Moved to Fri 8:15am · Thu 10am offered to waitlist

05

The problem

Recalls don't get done.

A patient cancels for a work trip or sickness and means to rebook but life get's in the way and they forget... Nobody follows up, because nobody has the time, and they quietly drop off your books.

The fix · Avilo AI

From $139/month →

Follow-ups that remember why they cancelled.

Avilo reads the reason they gave and checks in at the right time: after the trip, or a few days after they were sick. It offers their practitioner's free times and books them in when they reply. It also catches patients who've simply drifted.

Wed 6:48pm

Sorry, have to cancel Thursday. Away for work the rest of the week
No worries Mark, all cancelled. Safe travels!

Following Mon 9:00am

Hi Mark, hope the work trip went well. Priya has Wednesday 7:30am or Thursday 4pm free this week. Want one of those?
Thursday 4 please
Booked Thu 4:00pm with Priya

Off sick instead? Avilo checks in a few days later to see if they're feeling better and ready to rebook.

The problem

The same five questions, all day.

Parking, fees, what to bring, whether you take their health fund. Each one pulls someone away from the patient in front of them.

The fix · Avilo AI

From $139/month →

Answered from your clinic's own info.

Avilo only answers from what you've told it, never the open internet. Anything clinical or emotional goes straight to a person on your team, with no AI reply.
