How the integration works

Your Cliniko diary stays the single source of truth.

Avilo never asks you to run a second system. It connects through Cliniko's official API and works directly on the diary your team already lives in.

Practitioners, appointment types, booking rules and open slots import automatically, so Avilo only ever offers times that are genuinely free.

Cliniko questions

What Cliniko practices ask us.

Does Avilo use the official Cliniko API?

Yes. Avilo connects through Cliniko's official API, reading availability and writing appointments without moving patient data outside the Cliniko / Avilo perimeter. Your diary stays the single source of truth.

Does Avilo replace Cliniko's reminders?

No, it works with them. Cliniko keeps sending reminders exactly as it does today. Avilo handles everything that comes back: replies, reschedules, cancellations, confirmations and questions, writing every outcome back to the diary.

Will my team need to learn new software?

Barely. Your team keeps working in Cliniko as normal, and Avilo's work simply shows up there. Escalations arrive wherever your team already is: Slack, Teams, Discord or SMS.

How long does it take to connect?

One API key, automatic import, and a short call to confirm your policies. Most practices handle their first real conversation the same day.

We're not on Cliniko. Can we still use Avilo?

Avilo is built on Cliniko today. Support for other allied health practice management systems is on the roadmap. Tell us what you use; demand directly shapes what we build next.
