Security

Where your patients' data goes, and where it doesn't.

It's stored in Sydney, it's encrypted, and it's never used to train an AI model. Below is the full list of what Avilo can see and change in Cliniko, written so you don't need a lawyer to read it.

Where your data lives

Patient data is stored on Australian-hosted infrastructure.

  • Database and application hosted in Sydney
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Some processing happens overseas: AI language processing, voice, phone lines and staff email use providers in the United States
  • Every provider we use, and what it sees, is listed in our Privacy Policy

The Cliniko connection

Avilo connects through Cliniko’s official API, with a key you control.

  • Nothing installed in your browser or on your computers
  • Your key is encrypted and never shown again once entered
  • Revoke the key from Cliniko at any time and Avilo’s access stops
  • Avilo keeps only what it needs to do the job: patient names and contact details, appointments and the messages it handles

AI, kept on a short leash

On Inbox, most of the work is plain rules. Where AI is used, it’s constrained.

  • Your patient data is never used to train AI models
  • Clinical questions are never answered by AI
  • Anything clinical, urgent or unclear goes to your team
  • A staff reply pauses Avilo on that conversation
  • Opt-outs are handled by fixed rules before any AI sees the message

Your controls

It’s your clinic’s data, and it behaves that way.

  • Every conversation logged and reviewable
  • Avilo AI stays off until you switch it on
  • New features run in rehearsal first, so you see what would happen before anything is sent
  • Leaving? We export your data on request and delete it within 90 days

Plain list

What Avilo can see and change in Cliniko.

The short, complete list. If it isn't here, Avilo doesn't touch it.

What Avilo reads

  • Appointments, availability and the appointment note
  • Practitioners, appointment types and locations
  • Patient names, mobile numbers, emails and dates of birth, to match replies and confirm who’s calling
  • Replies to your Cliniko reminders

Avilo doesn't read clinical notes or treatment records.

What Avilo writes

  • Inbox: a confirmation note on the appointment. If you switch it on: cancelling an appointment after a clear "no" more than 24 hours out, and changing an appointment type so Cliniko sends a second reminder
  • Avilo AI, once switched on: bookings, reschedules and cancellations, class places, new patient records, and updated contact details a patient gives
  • A copy of each text in the patient’s Cliniko file, so your record stays complete

Nothing is written to Cliniko that a patient and your rules haven't agreed to.

Security questions.

Where is patient data stored?

In Australia. The database and application are hosted in Sydney, encrypted in transit and at rest. Some providers we use to process messages and calls are in the United States; the Privacy Policy lists each one.

Who can see patient messages?

The team members you invite to your clinic's Avilo account, and anyone in the email or chat channels you ask us to send alerts to. Avilo staff open a clinic's account only to set it up, support it or fix a fault, and each time is logged.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your patient data is never used to train models, ours or anyone else's.

Does Avilo record calls?

No call audio is kept. Avilo keeps a written transcript and summary in the conversation log, so your team can see what happened.

What happens if there's a data breach?

We notify affected clinics within 72 hours and work with you under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Do you have a Data Processing Agreement?

Yes. Every clinic gets it with their sign-on form. Email privacy@avilo.com.au for the full agreement and we'll send it the same business day.

Data Processing Agreement

Our Data Processing Agreement sets out exactly how we handle patient information for your clinic. In short:

  • We use patient information only to run Avilo for your clinic
  • No AI model is trained on it, and it's never shared between clinics
  • Every provider that touches it is named, and you get 30 days' notice before we add one
  • We tell you about a data breach within 72 hours
  • When you leave, you can export your data, and we delete it within 90 days

Want the full agreement? Email privacy@avilo.com.au and we’ll send it the same business day.

Questions about security, privacy or data handling? Email hello@avilo.com.au and you'll hear back from the founder, not a ticket queue.
