Why Avilo exists

Built by a physio, for allied health.

Avilo wasn't built by a SaaS team looking for a vertical. It was built by a physiotherapist who got tired of finding patient messages hours after they were sent, by which point the appointment was missed, the slot was empty, and the patient was annoyed.

Most of those messages weren't missed because we didn't care. They were missed because we were with patients, back-to-back, all day. That's the world Avilo is built for: software that knows what it's like to be on the floor of a clinic.

Common questions

Everything clinic owners ask before booking a demo.

How much does Avilo cost?

Pricing is tailored to your clinic. It depends on which features you want (calls, texting, recalls, waitlist and so on), your patient volume and the number of locations.

Rather than make you reverse-engineer a pricing table, we'll shape a package around your clinic on a short demo call. Book one here. No commitment, no pressure.

What does Avilo actually handle?

Two-way patient texting for reschedules, cancellations, confirmations and practice questions, with smart routing and escalation to your team. Inbound call answering, recalls & reactivation and waitlist auto-fill are rolling out soon. Everything writes back to your Cliniko diary in real time.

Anything clinical, emotional or ambiguous is escalated to the right person on your team with the full conversation attached. Routine handled, judgement kept human.

What happens when Avilo isn't sure how to respond?

Avilo only takes action on conversations where it's confident: reschedules, cancellations, simple practice questions. If a message is ambiguous, clinical or emotional, it stops and alerts you.

The moment a patient mentions pain levels, symptoms, treatment specifics, or anything off-script, Avilo hands the conversation to the right person on your team within 60 seconds. You can set the confidence threshold per practice. Conservative by default.

How long does setup take?

We connect Avilo to Cliniko via the official API, auto-import your practitioners, appointment types and booking rules, scan your website for FAQs and policies to confirm with you, and configure your routing. No engineering, no data migration, no downtime.

Most practices are running their first real patient conversation through Avilo by the end of the same morning.

Is my clinic's data safe with Avilo?

Yes. Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure. We integrate with Cliniko via their official API, reading availability and writing appointments without moving data outside the Cliniko / Avilo perimeter.

Conversations are encrypted in transit and at rest, we never train models on your patient data, and you can export or delete everything in one click.

Is Avilo trying to replace my admin team?

No. Avilo replaces the admin work nobody wants to do: the chase-up texts, the reschedule back-and-forth, the manual diary updates. For solo owners it hands back their evenings. For clinics with admin teams it frees them to spend their hours on patient relationships and the work that grows the practice.

Co-pilot, never replacement.

Does Avilo work with my practice management software?

Avilo is built on Cliniko today, with deep real-time integration via the official API. Support for other allied health practice management systems is on the roadmap.

If you're on something else, get in touch and tell us what you use. It directly shapes what we build next.
