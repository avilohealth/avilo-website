Why Avilo exists

Built by a physio, for allied health.

Avilo wasn't built by a SaaS team looking for a vertical. It was built by a physiotherapist who got tired of finding patient messages hours after they were sent, by which point the appointment was missed, the slot was empty, and the patient was annoyed.

Most of those messages weren't missed because we didn't care. They were missed because we were with patients, back-to-back, all day. That's the world Avilo is built for: software that knows what it's like to be on the floor of a clinic.

Common questions

What clinic owners ask before booking a demo.

How much does Avilo cost?

Plans start at $79 a month (ex GST) for reminders and confirmations, with the full receptionist (calls and texts) from $149 a month. Month-to-month, no setup fee, and you'll never pay more in usage than the next plan up. See pricing, or book a demo and we'll size the right plan from your real volumes.

What happens when Avilo isn't sure how to respond?

It stops. Anything ambiguous, clinical or emotional is handed to the right person on your team within 60 seconds, full conversation attached. The moment you type in a thread, Avilo pauses and hands it over. Conservative by default.

Is my clinic's data safe with Avilo?

Yes. Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest. We integrate with Cliniko via the official API, never train models on your patient data, and you can export or delete everything in one click.

Is Avilo trying to replace my admin team?

No. It replaces the admin work nobody wants (chase-up texts, reschedule back-and-forth, manual diary updates) so your team spends its hours on the patient at the desk, not the inbox. Co-pilot, never replacement.
