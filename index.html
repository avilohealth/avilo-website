How it works

Every reply, handled in seconds. Without you lifting a finger.

When a patient replies, Avilo reads the message, figures out what they want, and conducts the entire conversation via SMS. It checks real availability in your Cliniko diary, presents options, confirms the booking, and updates everything in the background.

Most patients have no idea anything happened on your end. They just got a fast, helpful reply.

The growth feature

Cliniko has Recalls... But doesn't tell you when patients reply.

Recalls are built into Cliniko. You can mark a patient for follow-up, send a message, and… nothing else. No tracking. No response handling. No way to know if they replied. No bookings unless someone manually chases them.

That's not a recall system. That's a one-way broadcast with a follow-up list.

The patient list, automatically built.

Avilo finds every patient without an upcoming appointment — across your whole practice, filtered by practitioner, last-seen window, or appointment type.

Personalised at scale.
