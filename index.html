What clinics ask us.

Where is patient data stored?

In Australia. Patient data Avilo processes is hosted on Australian servers. How we secure it →

What can Avilo see and change in Cliniko?

Reads: appointments, practitioners, appointment types, patient contact details, and replies to your reminders.

Writes: confirmation status and appointment notes. Once Avilo AI is on, also bookings, reschedules and cancellations.

Who can see patient messages?

The team members you invite to your clinic's Avilo account. Avilo staff only open a conversation when you ask us to for support.

How do you handle health information and privacy?

We collect only what's needed to manage appointments and messages, and handle it under our Privacy Policy and the Australian Privacy Principles. Read the Privacy Policy →

Does Avilo ever reply to patients without us?

Only once you switch on Avilo AI, and only within your rules. Anything clinical, emotional or unclear comes to your team.

Is an AI receptionist replacing our reception team?

No. It works alongside them, handling the routine replies, after-hours calls and confirmations so your team can look after the people in front of them.

Which channels does Avilo answer?

Text and phone calls today. Email and website chat are coming soon, answered by the same receptionist with the same rules.

How long does setup take?

One 30-minute call. We connect Cliniko and set your rules with you.

How much does it cost?

Texts only starts at $49 a month, with Automate at $99 and Multi-site at $179. Adding calls starts at $139 a month, then $299 and $649 as volume grows. Every plan has unlimited practitioners, month to month, with no setup fee. See pricing →

Can we cancel?

Yes. Avilo is month-to-month, with no setup fee.
