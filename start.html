Before you start

Quick answers.

Is it really free for 14 days?

Yes. No card, no setup fee. We email you on day 12. If you want to keep going, you add a payment method. If not, the trial ends and your Cliniko account is untouched.

Will Avilo send anything to my patients?

Not on Confirmations. It only reads the replies to the reminders Cliniko already sends. Sending is part of Smart Reminders, which you can move up to later.

Is my patient data safe?

Patient data is hosted in Australia, encrypted in transit and at rest, and never used to train AI models. How we handle it.
