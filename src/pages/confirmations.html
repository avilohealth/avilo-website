Where the rules end and the AI begins

Most of Confirmations is plain rules. A yes is a yes. A no is a no. When a reply isn't clear, a small AI step reads it, and if it still isn't sure, it goes to your inbox for a person. Nothing clinical is ever answered by software. How we handle patient data.

Questions

What clinics ask about Confirmations.

How does Avilo read replies if Cliniko sends the reminders?

Patients reply to Cliniko's reminder as normal. Avilo connects to your Cliniko account through the official API, reads each reply as it arrives, works out whether it's a yes, a no, a question or something unclear, and writes the result to the appointment note and the Avilo diary.

Can I reply to a patient from Avilo?

On Confirmations you read each patient's full message history in Avilo and click through to their Cliniko profile to reply, because Cliniko's API allows SMS to be read but not sent. Smart Reminders adds sending from your clinic's own Avilo number.

Is the waitlist Cliniko's waitlist?

No. Cliniko's waitlist isn't available through its API, so Avilo keeps its own. Your team adds patients who want in sooner, and when a matching slot opens Avilo alerts the team to contact them.

What does a clear no do?

That's your rule. Avilo can cancel the appointment in Cliniko straight away so the slot is free, or it can flag the reply for a person to action. Most clinics choose instant cancel for plain replies like "No" or "Can't make it", and flag anything with a question attached.

How much of this is AI?

Not much. Most replies are read by plain rules. When a reply is unclear, a small AI step reads it, and if it still isn't sure it's marked as needing a person. Nothing clinical is ever answered by software.

What do I need to start?

A Cliniko account with SMS reminders switched on, an API key from a Cliniko admin user, and ten minutes. Nothing is installed and nothing changes for patients.
