Pricing questions.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! 14 days free, no credit card. We set it up with you on one 30-minute call, and if you don't keep going, the trial ends and your Cliniko account is untouched.

What counts as a reminder text?

Each text Avilo sends to nudge or chase a patient about an upcoming appointment, counted per SMS segment. Replies from patients are never charged.

What if we go over our Avilo AI allowance?

Extra calls are $2.50 each and extra texts are 7.5c. You'll never pay more in usage than the next plan up: if you would, we move you across automatically.

Can we switch plans later?

Yes, anytime. Moving between plans, or adding calls, needs no new setup; your rules and history carry over. You can move back down too.

Is there a contract?

No. Everything is month-to-month. Cancel anytime and it ends at the close of the current billing cycle.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Setup is one 30-minute call with us, and it's included.

What if we have more than one location?

Every plan has unlimited practitioners. For texts only, Multi-site covers several sites with a dashboard for each. With calls, Scale suits groups and high call volume. Tell us your setup on the demo and we'll size it with you.
