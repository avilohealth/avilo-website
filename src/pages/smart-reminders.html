Questions

What clinics ask about Smart Reminders.

Do patients get texts from a different number?

Yes. On Smart Reminders, reminders and follow-ups come from your clinic's own Avilo number, and patients reply to that number. We can also port an existing clinic mobile number if you have one.

Does the AI write replies on Smart Reminders?

No. Avilo sends the scheduled messages: reminders, follow-up nudges and the day-before chase. Every conversation reply is written by your team from the Avilo inbox. AI replies are part of the Avilo Assistant.

Can each practitioner have their own inbox?

Yes. Each practitioner can see and reply to messages from their own patients, while the front desk sees everything. No shared clinic phone, no forwarding screenshots.

Do I need to turn off Cliniko's reminders?

Yes, once Smart Reminders is live, so patients don't get two. We walk you through the switch, and appointment changes still write back to Cliniko as before.
