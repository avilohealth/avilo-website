Before you book

Quick answers to the usual questions.

Do I need a demo to start Confirmations?

No. Confirmations is a 14-day free trial you can start from the trial page. We'll have you set up within one business day and you can book a setup call if you'd like a hand.

What happens on the demo call?

Thirty minutes, no sales script. We look at how your front desk runs today, show you Avilo handling real scenarios on a live clinic diary, and answer everything on the spot. If it's not a fit, we'll tell you that too.

How long does it take to go live?

Confirmations: ten minutes with a Cliniko API key. Smart Reminders and the Assistant: a short setup together to confirm your FAQs, policies and routing. Most practices handle their first real conversation the same day.

We're not on Cliniko. Can we still use Avilo?

Avilo is built on Cliniko today. Support for other allied health practice management systems is on the roadmap. Send an enquiry and tell us what you use; demand shapes what we build next.

Is patient data safe?

Yes. Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest. We never train models on your patient data, and you can export or delete everything. More on security.
