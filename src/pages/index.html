Common questions

What clinics ask before they start the trial.

Does anything change for my patients?

No. Your reminders keep going out from Cliniko exactly as they do now, from the same number. Avilo reads the replies that come back. Patients never see a new number or a new message on this layer.

Do I have to stop using Cliniko's reminders?

No. Avilo Confirmations works with Cliniko's reminders, not instead of them. If you later move up to Smart Reminders, Avilo sends the reminders and follow-ups for you, but that is a choice, not a requirement.

What happens to a reply Avilo can't read?

It goes to a person. Most replies are clear and handled by plain rules. When one isn't, a small AI step reads it, and if it still isn't sure, the message is marked as needing a person and lands at the top of your inbox.

Can I reply to patients from Avilo?

On Confirmations, Avilo shows you each patient's message history in one readable thread and gives you a one-click link to their Cliniko profile to reply, because Cliniko's API lets us read SMS but not send it. Smart Reminders adds sending from your clinic's Avilo number.

What does the free trial include?

Everything in Avilo Confirmations for 14 days: the colour-coded diary, the morning call list, instant cancel on a no, the patient inbox, waitlist alerts and the Monday cancelled-without-rebook list. No credit card needed.

What happens after 14 days?

We email you on day 12. If you want to keep going, you add a payment method and nothing else changes. If you don't, the trial simply ends and your Cliniko account is untouched.
