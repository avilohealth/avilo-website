Your patients' data, treated like it matters.

Avilo touches sensitive information every day. Here's exactly how it's protected, in plain language.

Where your data lives

Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Database and application hosted in Sydney
  • Access limited to what the product needs to run
  • Every provider we use is listed in our Privacy Policy

The Cliniko connection

Avilo integrates with Cliniko through the official API, with a key you control.

  • Reads replies and availability, writes notes and appointment changes
  • Nothing installed in your browser or on your computers
  • Revoke the key from Cliniko at any time
  • No copying your patient database anywhere else

AI, kept on a short leash

On Confirmations, most of the work is plain rules. Where AI is used, it is constrained.

  • Models are never trained on your patient data
  • Clinical questions are never answered by AI
  • Anything unclear goes to a person
  • Replying in a thread pauses the Assistant on that conversation

Your controls

It's your clinic's data, and it behaves that way.

  • Export or delete everything
  • Every conversation logged and reviewable
  • You approve outbound campaigns before they send
  • You set the rules: cancel on no, who gets alerts, escalation thresholds

This website sets no tracking cookies and sends HSTS, content-type and referrer-policy headers. Questions about security, privacy or data handling? Email hello@avilo.com.au and you'll hear back from the founder, not a ticket queue. See also our Privacy Policy.
