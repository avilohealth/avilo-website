How a physio front desk runs

Most of your diary is someone's third, fourth or fifth visit.

A physiotherapy clinic lives on courses of treatment. An initial consult becomes a plan of four to eight follow-ups, often weekly or twice weekly, and every one of those visits gets its own Cliniko reminder. Across a team of practitioners, the desk is reading replies to appointments booked weeks ago, for patients who may have improved, flared up, or forgotten which Thursday they agreed to.

The case mix makes it harder. Sports patients cancel when the knee feels fine and when training moves. Workers compensation patients turn up reliably but reply with questions about approvals and paperwork. Post-operative patients reply with symptoms. All of it lands in the same list, and someone has to work out which replies are a yes, which are a no, and which need a phone call.

What matters most in physio

Three features that earn their place in a physio clinic.

  • Colour-coded diaryWith six follow-ups per patient, confirmations arrive in a steady stream all week. Each practitioner's column coloured by reply means the desk stops cross-checking a message list against the diary.
  • Instant cancel on a noPhysio slots are short and booked well ahead, so a no at 7pm is worth far more than a no found at 9am. Avilo can cancel the appointment in Cliniko the moment a plain no arrives, while someone can still take the slot.
  • Waitlist alertsA patient with a fresh injury wants to be seen this week, not next. When a follow-up falls out of the diary, Avilo tells the desk who asked for that practitioner on those days.

Questions

What physio clinics ask.

We book a whole course of treatment up front. What happens to a reply that covers several appointments?

Each Cliniko reminder is tied to one appointment, so Avilo marks the appointment that reminder was for. If a patient writes that they want to cancel all their sessions, Avilo does not cancel anything in bulk. It flags the reply as needing a person so the desk can speak to the patient and the physio before touching the rest of the plan.

Our workers compensation patients ask about approvals in their replies. What happens?

Avilo reads the reply, sees that it is a question rather than a yes or a no, and places it in the inbox as needing a person. Nothing about claims, approvals or paperwork is answered by software. The patient's thread and a link to their Cliniko profile sit together, so whoever handles claims can reply from Cliniko.

Can each physio see confirmations for their own patients only?

Yes. The diary and the morning call list filter by practitioner, so a physio who checks their own column before the first patient sees only their patients. On Smart Reminders each practitioner can also have their own inbox.

Does Avilo change how our reminders are worded or timed?

No. On Confirmations, Cliniko keeps sending the reminders you already have, at the times you already set, and Avilo only reads the replies. If you want Avilo to send the reminders and a day-before follow-up, that is Smart Reminders.
