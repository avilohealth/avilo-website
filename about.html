About Avilo

Hi, I'm Keegan. I'm a physio, and I built Avilo.

I kept finding patient texts hours after they came in, usually between back-to-backs and usually too late to save the slot. So I built something to catch them for me and my team. That turned into Avilo.

Why Avilo exists

The problem nobody warned me about.

I work in a busy practice. Keeping on top of who's responded to a reminder is a constant, tedious task. You're treating back-to-back all day. A patient texts back to reschedule, replying to the reminder that literally says "do not reply", because everyone messages these days, and you don't see it for hours.

They don't show up. You've got a gap in your diary. Worse, if a couple of days pass before anyone replies, the patient thinks you ignored them. That's not just a missed appointment. It's a slot that could have been filled, and a patient relationship that's a little colder than it needs to be.

I kept hearing the same thing from colleagues at other practices. The problem isn't Cliniko. Its reminders are excellent. The problem is that when a patient replies, nothing is built to catch it. Confirm is that missing piece, and it's where most clinics start.

Why I'm the one building this

A physio who can't stop tinkering.

I'm a physio first. But I've always loved the way a small system tweak can give a whole team hours back. I built the first version of Avilo for me and my team, to take the admin off our plates between sessions.

What I didn't expect was how much better it made things for patients. Practices with a reception team benefit most: receptionists stop watching incoming messages all day and get back to being the welcoming face patients came in for.

Avilo has been accepted into the ElevenLabs Grants program, which backs the voice technology behind Avilo's after-hours call answering.

What Avilo believes

Four principles that shape every decision.

01

Start where it's easy.

One 30-minute call, nothing for patients to notice. If the first step needs a project plan, it's the wrong first step.

02

Speed is care.

A patient who replies and hears nothing for six hours doesn't feel valued. They feel forgotten. Responding fast is how trust gets built.

03

Confidence beats coverage.

Avilo only handles what it's certain about. Anything ambiguous, clinical or emotional pulls a person in. A confident "I don't know" beats a confident wrong answer.

04

Alongside your team, not instead of it.

Every feature gets one question: does this give the front desk their time back? If it doesn't, it doesn't ship.
