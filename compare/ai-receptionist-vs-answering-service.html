AI receptionist or answering service: which one suits your clinic?

One is a person reading from a script. The other is software reading your live Cliniko diary. Here is what each does well, what each cannot do, and when the other is the better choice.

The two options

They sound similar. They do different jobs.

An answering service puts a person on the phone when your team cannot be. An AI receptionist answers calls and texts against your live Cliniko diary.

A human answering service

A team of operators, local or offshore, answers your clinic's number under your name, from a script you supply, and passes messages back to you.

Does well

  • Picks up every call within your contracted hours, with a human voice
  • Handles a flustered or upset caller with real patience
  • Takes a clear message and sends it on by email or text
  • Follows a script for the common questions: hours, parking, what to bring

Cannot do

  • Book, move or cancel an appointment in Cliniko. Most services only take a message
  • See your reminder replies, missed texts or waitlist
  • Know a patient's history or which practitioner they usually see
  • Cover texts at all. Patients who reply to a reminder are still your job

An AI receptionist (Avilo)

Software connected to your Cliniko account that answers calls and texts and books against the live diary.

Does well

  • Answers calls and texts 24/7, in your clinic's tone
  • Reschedules into real gaps and writes the change back to Cliniko
  • Reads every reminder reply and frees the slot on a no
  • Offers a cancelled slot to the waitlist

Cannot do

  • Answer a clinical question. Ever. Those go to a practitioner
  • Read tone the way a person can. An unclear message goes to your inbox
  • Work outside your knowledge base. If the answer is not there, a person gets it
  • Book into any system other than Cliniko

Being fair

When a human answering service is the better choice.

We would rather you pick the right tool than pick us.

Your callers need a lot of nuance

Some clinics see a high share of distressed, elderly or non-English-speaking callers. A patient operator handles that better than any script or software.

Your calls are conversations, not bookings

If most calls are insurer queries, care coordination or families working through a plan, a trained person on the line will serve them better than software built around the diary.

You do not use Cliniko

Avilo is built for Cliniko clinics. If your diary lives somewhere else, Avilo cannot book into it.

You want a person's name on the phone

For some practices the human voice is part of the brand, and no automation should take that away.

Questions

What clinics ask when weighing the two.

Can an answering service book into Cliniko?

Most cannot. Operators generally do not have access to your diary, so they take a message for your team to action the next morning. A few will log in to a booking system if you hand over access, which is a decision about who sees patient data. Avilo books directly against the live diary through the Cliniko API.

Does Avilo answer phone calls?

Yes, on the texts and calls plans. Avilo answers 24/7, books, reschedules and cancels in Cliniko during the call, and passes anything clinical to a person. Call audio is not kept.

What happens when Avilo is not sure what a patient means?

It stops. The message and the full thread go to your inbox, and whoever you nominate is alerted within a minute. It never guesses and never gives clinical advice.

Can my team step in on a conversation?

Yes. Reply in a thread and Avilo AI pauses there. It checks with the clinic before it picks the thread back up, so a patient never gets two answers.

Can I use an answering service and Avilo together?

Yes, and some clinics do. The service covers the phone while Avilo covers texts, reminder replies and the diary. If the service logs a message asking to move an appointment, your team actions it in Cliniko and Avilo picks up the change from there.

Where is patient data kept?

In Sydney, encrypted in transit and at rest. It is never used to train AI models, and no call audio is kept.
