5. What the Service does

What you receive depends on your Plan and your settings.

5.1 Confirmations features. Avilo reads your PMS appointments and your Patients' replies to the reminders your PMS sends, works out what each reply means, and shows your team who has confirmed, who has not and who needs a reply. Where you ask us to switch them on, Avilo can also: write a confirmation note to the appointment; cancel an appointment when a Patient clearly replies "no" more than 24 hours beforehand; change an appointment's type so your PMS sends a second reminder; and match waitlisted Patients to freed times. On these features Avilo does not send or reply to any patient message.

5.2 Messaging features. Avilo sends appointment reminders and confirmation requests from a number we provide, and gives your team a shared inbox to reply from, with waitlist, recall and campaign tools.

5.3 AI texting. Where switched on, Avilo replies to Patients by text to book, move and cancel appointments, answer the practice questions you have approved, offer freed times to your waitlist, and writes those changes to your PMS.

5.4 AI calls. Where included in your Plan, an AI assistant answers calls to book, move and cancel appointments, answer approved practice questions, or take a message. Calls are not audio-recorded; a written transcript and summary are kept.

5.5 New patients. Where a person who is not yet your Patient calls or messages, Avilo may create a patient record in your PMS and book an appointment, recording a brief reason for the visit.

5.6 Hand-off. Avilo hands a conversation to your team when it appears clinical, urgent or unclear, when the Patient asks for a person, or when Avilo cannot complete the request. Hand-offs appear in the Service and are sent to the notification channels you set up. A staff reply pauses Avilo on that conversation.

5.7 Dependencies. The Service depends on your PMS's API, on telecommunications carriers and on our providers. Avilo is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any PMS. New or beta features may be offered on different terms that we tell you about when you switch them on.

6. Safety features

6.1 The following are built into the Service and are not settings:

(a) every AI-answered call begins by telling the caller they are speaking with an AI assistant;

(b) opt-out keywords such as OPT-OUT and STOP are processed by fixed rules before any AI handling, and a Patient who opts out receives no further recall, check-in or campaign texts from the Service;

(c) messages that appear clinical or urgent, and requests for a person, are handed to your team and not answered by AI;

(d) call audio is not recorded.

6.2 Settings that change what the Service does to your diary or sends to your Patients (for example going live, rehearsal mode, automatic cancellation and reminders) are changed only on your instruction. Each change is confirmed and logged with who made it, when and why, and the log is available to you.

6.3 New features that act on your diary run in rehearsal first, so you can see what would have happened before anything is sent or changed.
