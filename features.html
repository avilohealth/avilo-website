Safety net

Smart triage & escalation

A patient in more pain who wants in sooner gets an earlier appointment — booked on the spot, with the therapist briefed on what's changed. A purely clinical message — symptoms, treatment questions — is never answered by AI. It goes straight to the therapist.

  • Urgent reschedules get the patient in sooner — not just flagged
  • Therapist briefed automatically whenever a patient reports worsening symptoms
  • Purely clinical questions are never answered by AI — routed straight to the therapist
  • Alerts land in Slack, Teams, Discord or SMS within 60 seconds, full thread attached
Conservative by default — you set the threshold for your clinic.
