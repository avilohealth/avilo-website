Safety net

Smart triage & escalation

A patient in more pain who wants in sooner gets an earlier appointment, booked on the spot with the therapist briefed on what's changed. A purely clinical message, like symptoms or treatment questions, is never answered by AI. It goes straight to the therapist.

  • Urgent reschedules get the patient in sooner, not just flagged
  • Therapist briefed automatically whenever a patient reports worsening symptoms
  • Purely clinical questions are never answered by AI. They go straight to the therapist
  • Alerts land in Slack, Teams, Discord or SMS within 60 seconds, full thread attached
Conservative by default. You set the threshold for your clinic.
