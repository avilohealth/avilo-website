How the integration works

Your Cliniko diary stays the single source of truth.

Avilo never asks you to run a second system. Your team keeps working the way they do now.

Reads replies and availability

Reminder replies, practitioners, appointment types, locations and open slots come in through the API. Avilo only works with what's genuinely in your diary.

Writes back where your team looks

Confirmation status goes into the appointment. Once Avilo AI is on, reschedules, cancellations and bookings land in the diary the moment a conversation resolves.

Data stays in Australia

Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure, is never used to train models, and your API key can be revoked from Cliniko at any time.

What Cliniko practices ask us.

Does Avilo use the official Cliniko API?

Yes. Avilo connects through Cliniko's official API with a key you create under an admin user. Nothing is installed in your browser or on your computers, and you can revoke the key at any time from Cliniko. Avilo is an independent product and isn't endorsed by Cliniko.

Does Avilo replace Cliniko's reminders?

No. Cliniko keeps sending reminders exactly as it does today. Avilo reads the replies, shows who's confirmed, and chases the quiet ones.

What does Avilo write into Cliniko?

On Confirm, confirmation status goes into the appointment. Once Avilo AI is on, confirmed reschedules, cancellations and new bookings are written to the diary as they happen.

Does Avilo use Cliniko's waitlist?

No. Cliniko's waitlist isn't available through its API, so Avilo keeps its own. Your team adds patients who want in sooner, and Avilo offers them freed slots once Avilo AI is on.

What if my clinic isn't on Cliniko?

Avilo works with Cliniko today. Tell us what you use in the form above and we'll let you know if that changes.

Getting connected

Where do I find my API key?

Three steps, about two minutes. You'll need to be logged in to Cliniko as an admin user.

Screenshot 1: My Info → Manage API keys
1

Open your API keys in Cliniko

Log in as an admin user. Click your name (bottom left) to open My Info, then choose Manage API keys.

Screenshot 2: naming and copying the key
2

Add a key and copy it

Click Add an API key, name it "Avilo", and copy the key Cliniko shows you. It's only shown once.

Screenshot 3: pasting the key into Avilo
3

Paste it into Avilo

Paste the key into Avilo on your setup call. Practitioners, appointment types and locations import on their own.

You can revoke the key from Cliniko at any time, and Avilo stops reading straight away. How we protect your data →
