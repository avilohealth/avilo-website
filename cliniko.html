How the integration works

Cliniko questions

What Cliniko practices ask us.

Does Avilo use the official Cliniko API?

Yes. Avilo connects through Cliniko's official API with a key you create under an admin user. Nothing is installed in your browser or on your computers, and you can revoke the key at any time from Cliniko.

Does Avilo replace Cliniko's reminders?

Not on Confirmations. Cliniko keeps sending reminders exactly as it does today and Avilo reads the replies. If you move up to Smart Reminders, Avilo sends the reminders and follow-ups instead, so patients only ever get one set.

What does Avilo write into Cliniko?

On Confirmations, the reply status goes into the appointment note, and a clear no can cancel the appointment if you choose. On Smart Reminders and the Assistant, confirmed reschedules, cancellations and new bookings are written to the diary as they happen.

Can Avilo reply to SMS through Cliniko?

No. Cliniko's API allows SMS to be read but not sent, so on Confirmations your team replies from the patient's Cliniko profile, one click from the Avilo inbox. Smart Reminders and the Assistant send from your clinic's own Avilo number.

Does Avilo use Cliniko's waitlist?

No. Cliniko's waitlist isn't exposed through its API, so Avilo keeps its own. Your team adds patients who want in sooner and Avilo alerts the team, or on the Assistant offers the slot, when a match opens.

What if my clinic isn't on Cliniko?

Avilo is built on Cliniko today. Support for other allied health practice management systems is on the roadmap. Tell us what you use and we'll let you know when it's supported.
