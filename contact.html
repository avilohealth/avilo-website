Before you book

Quick answers to the usual questions.

How much does Avilo cost?

Pricing depends on which features you want (calls, texting, confirmations, recalls, waitlist) plus your patient volume and number of locations. On the demo we'll work out what your clinic actually needs and quote a package around that.

No lock-in surprises. You'll know the number before you commit to anything.

What happens on the demo call?

Thirty minutes, no sales script. We'll look at how your front desk runs today, show you Avilo handling real scenarios on a live clinic diary (reschedules, confirmations, escalations, calls) and answer everything on the spot.

If it's not a fit, we'll tell you that too.

How long does it take to go live?

We connect to Cliniko via the official API, import your practitioners and booking rules, confirm your FAQs and policies with you, and configure routing. Most practices handle their first real conversation the same day.

We're not on Cliniko. Can we still use Avilo?

Avilo integrates deeply with Cliniko today, and support for other allied health practice management systems is on the roadmap. Send us an enquiry and tell us what you use. Demand directly shapes what we build next, and we'll let you know as soon as your system is supported.

Is patient data safe?

Yes. Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest. We never train models on your patient data, and you can export or delete everything in one click.
