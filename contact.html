Quick answers to the usual questions.

What happens on the demo call?

Thirty minutes, no sales script. We look at how your front desk runs today, show you Avilo on a live Cliniko diary, and answer everything on the spot. If it's not a fit, we'll tell you that too.

How long does it take to go live?

One 30-minute call. We connect Cliniko and set your rules with you. Most clinics see their first confirmation list the same day.

We're not on Cliniko. Can we still use Avilo?

Avilo works with Cliniko today. Send an enquiry and tell us what you use; demand shapes what we build next.

Is patient data safe?

Patient data stays on Australian-hosted infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest. We never train models on your patient data, and you can export or delete everything. More on security →
